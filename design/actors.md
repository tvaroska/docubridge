# Actors — who does what

> Five actors take part in every exchange. Each owns a small number of decisions, is forbidden a small number of others, and has that line enforced somewhere in code. Use these names; the aliases in the last column are retired.

The line that matters most runs between the first two: the **calling agent** decides what is needed and when it has enough; **DocuBridge** decides whether each document that arrives is what it claims to be. Neither decision may be minted by a model. The other three actors are the people and systems on the far side of the Bridge — the ones who send documents, unblock the cases the gate cannot settle, and teach the system when it was wrong.

Two consequences worth stating plainly:

- **Reviewer and domain expert are two roles, not two people.** The same underwriter resolves a parked payoff letter (reviewer) and, in the same screen, says "this kind of waiver is always fine when the statement shows PAID IN FULL" (domain expert). The first act changes one case; the second enters Calibration and is gated, versioned and reversible. The surfaces keep the two acts distinguishable — blind pre-commitment, dwell time and citation checks exist so that a reviewer's click cannot silently become a lesson.
- **The calling agent is the only actor that can say "done" or "waived."** DocuBridge judges documents and concludes sufficiency with evidence, providers supply, reviewers unblock and recommend, experts teach — none of them completes an exchange or drops a requirement. The line is evidence versus judgment (ADR-14): DocuBridge decides what follows from the documents plus the declared strategy; the calling agent decides what needs context the documents do not contain. This is why a downstream showcase needs no change to DocuBridge to add a new document set: completeness logic lives with the calling agent and the process skill it names.

Actors not covered here — the platform operator who installs content and deploys, the governance approver for Calibration promotions where that is a distinct person from the domain expert, the downstream showcase owner, and the auditor who reads the vault and lineage — are described where their surfaces are: [consuming over A2A](../docs/consume-over-a2a.md), [Calibration](knowledge-model.md), [documents as artifacts](artifacts.md).
