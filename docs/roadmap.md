# Roadmap (`docubridge`)

Historical architectural decisions live in [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md), non-negotiable invariants in [CRITICAL.md](../CRITICAL.md), feature definitions in [features.md](features.md), and design specifications under [design/bridge.md](../design/bridge.md).

## Where we are

`docubridge` (`v1.19.0`) ships a 6-Zone governed A2A document mediation core across all 14 dual-mode (`local`/`gcp`) Hexagonal `Port` boundaries (`sessions`, `task_store`, `exchange_store`, `skill_registry`, `memory_bank`, `scheduler`, `a2a_client`, `extraction`, `email_ingress`, `audit_vault`, `operational_store`, `action_inbox`, `agent_gym`, `knowledge_catalog`), backed by `2,017` passing unit/contract tests (`>=90%` coverage), `57` credential-free `terraform test` checks, executable `specs/human/` contract checks, Schema Refinery (`P8-1`–`P8-8`), generic `ScenarioEngine` (`P9-1`–`P9-3`), **`P10` (`v1.15.0`)** security/IaC/SQL/supply-chain hardening, **`P11` (`v1.16.0`)** declarative collection strategy (`ADR-14`/`ADR-15`) and canonical A2A v1.0 counterparty dial-out, **`P12` (`v1.17.0`)** Tier-2 `ui-design` governance and zero standards debt, **`P13` (`v1.18.0`)** deciding-code hardening (`ADR-16`), and **`P14` (`v1.19.0`)** deployed-surface & caller-allowlist hardening (`850f034`). Milestones `P10`–`P14` are shipped and archived under `## Shipped`; `P15` (2026-11-21 test/gate findings) and `P16` (2026-11-22 `/top3-audit` onboarding, compiler, SQL & egress findings) are open.
